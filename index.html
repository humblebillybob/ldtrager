About Lisa

I've spent two decades building organizations, raising capital, and leading teams through complexity — always with an eye toward what the work ultimately makes possible.

What draws me to complex work is the clarity it demands. I bring strategic thinking grounded in operational reality, and leadership that holds up under scrutiny.
